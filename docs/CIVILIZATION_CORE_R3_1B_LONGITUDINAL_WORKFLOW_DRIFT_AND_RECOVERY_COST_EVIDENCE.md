## 3. Corpus, Method, and Counting Rules

The corpus covers one Human Owner and one long-running Civilization Core project. It contains four distinct task-supplied incident records observed across the route from a pre-R1 correction through completion of R3.1A. The evidence is longitudinal founder/operator evidence, not independent-user evidence.

The method is direct incident reconstruction followed by comparison with repository-recorded route expectations and predecessor states. It uses observable-count evidence only. It includes no questionnaire, external research, prototype, or implemented-product test. It makes no elapsed-time or monetary estimate and no technical-cause inference.

For this artifact, one incident is one distinct proposed action or route transition that required a Human Owner correction before correct progression resumed. Separate corrections are counted separately only when they address separate proposed actions or route transitions. Counts describe observed events in this bounded corpus; they do not estimate prevalence, duration, labor, financial impact, or market demand.

## 9. Question-by-Question Dispositions

### LQ1: Is project-route drift recurrent in the observed workflow?

- **Supporting evidence:** Four distinct incidents across the route from pre-R1 correction through R3.1A each required Human Owner correction.
- **Counterevidence:** The documentation helped identify and restore the expected route, and the corpus does not establish whether every project segment drifted.
- **Uncertainty and limitations:** One project, one Human Owner, no prevalence estimate, and no established independence or common technical cause.
- **Disposition:** `PASS`.
- **Rationale:** Recurrence is established within this bounded corpus only; the disposition is not a universal or market-level claim.

### LQ2: Does the Human Owner bear observable recovery burden?

- **Supporting evidence:** Four correction interventions, one full roadmap repost, route clarification, repeated state restatement, interrupted attention, and loss of forward momentum are observed.
- **Counterevidence:** Repository controls aided correction, and no quantitative cost was measured.
- **Uncertainty and limitations:** Duration, labor, monetary cost, opportunity cost, and magnitude of trust degradation are unknown.
- **Disposition:** `PASS`.
- **Rationale:** Observable correction burden is established, while its quantitative cost is not.

### LQ3: Do repository documentation and route controls alone prevent drift?

- **Supporting evidence:** All four deviations occurred in a workflow with repository documentation or recorded route expectations available; LD-3 required reposting the controlling roadmap.
- **Counterevidence:** Documentation enabled comparison, correction, and route recovery, and LD-1 preceded adoption of the post-IDG master roadmap.
- **Uncertainty and limitations:** The corpus does not compare controlled and uncontrolled conditions or test all documentary controls; universal prevention effectiveness cannot be inferred.
- **Disposition:** `HOLD`.
- **Rationale:** Documentation alone did not prevent drift in this corpus, but universal documentary-control failure is neither tested nor claimed.

### LQ4: Is project continuity and anti-drift control a material operator requirement?

- **Supporting evidence:** Recurrent route deviation, four Human Owner corrections, a full roadmap repost, and explicit rejection of ceremonial confirmation show material continuity pain in the observed workflow.
- **Counterevidence:** The evidence comes from one Human Owner and one project and does not test an implemented control.
- **Uncertainty and limitations:** Prevalence, preferred product mechanism, willingness to pay, and comparative benefit are unknown.
- **Disposition:** `PASS`.
- **Rationale:** The requirement signal is material for this founder/operator workflow only and is a candidate for later synthesis, not broad validation.

### LQ5: Does this longitudinal evidence validate the five R2 workflows?

- **Supporting evidence:** Provenance, human correction, route state, and traceability concerns are adjacent to parts of the selected direction.
- **Counterevidence:** The corpus did not exercise or evaluate all five R2 workflows and did not directly test the evidence/candidate/review/approval/adoption/execution distinctions.
- **Uncertainty and limitations:** No workflow walkthrough, independent user observation, usability evaluation, reversibility test, or implemented-product use occurred.
- **Disposition:** `NOT-TESTED`.
- **Rationale:** Adjacent support for continuity and anti-drift needs cannot substitute for testing the five workflows.

### LQ6: Does this evidence establish comparative Control Plane value?

- **Supporting evidence:** The observed burden supplies a problem signal that a future governed continuity control might address.
- **Counterevidence:** No implemented Control Plane, comparison condition, measurement, or ungoverned-workflow evaluation exists.
- **Uncertainty and limitations:** Effectiveness, overhead, comparative value, adoption, market demand, and willingness to pay are unknown.
- **Disposition:** `NOT-TESTED`.
- **Rationale:** A problem signal is not comparative product-value evidence.
