## 14. Deterministic rationale-completeness rubric

The exact scores are:

- **0:** blank, unrelated, or no usable reason.
- **1:** states an outcome but identifies no material evidence or boundary.
- **2:** identifies one relevant fact or boundary.
- **3:** identifies the relevant fact/boundary and explains why it supports
  the outcome.
- **4:** does everything required for 3 and explicitly preserves the
  non-applied, no-promotion, or fail-closed consequence where relevant.

Implementation must be deterministic and based on explicit required reason
codes or operator-selected rubric fields. No LLM, model, semantic-similarity
service, or subjective automated prose grading is authorized.

## 16. Exact learning-decision rules

The valid completed evaluation must assign exactly one result.

### 16.1 `LEARNING-SUPPORTS-CONTINUED-GOVERNED-EVALUATION`

This result is permitted only if all of the following are true:

- governed decision correctness is at least baseline correctness;
- governed decision correctness is at least five of six;
- governed critical detection is five of five;
- governed state-promotion misunderstandings are zero;
- governed median rationale completeness is at least baseline;
- governed correction/rework total is no greater than baseline;
- governed median human completion time is no greater than 150 percent of
  baseline;
- governed median operator action count is no more than two actions above
  baseline;
- governed perceived usefulness is at least four of five; and
- governed perceived governance burden is at most three of five.

### 16.2 `LEARNING-GAIN-WITH-BURDEN-HOLD`

This result applies only when governed safety or decision quality improves,
but one or more burden, time, action-count, correction, usefulness, or
burden-score thresholds above fail.

### 16.3 `NO-SUPPORT-FOR-EXPANSION`

This result applies when governed safety and decision quality do not improve
and the governed condition is equal or worse in burden.

### 16.4 `INVALID-MEASUREMENT`

This result is mandatory when:

- any trial is missing;
- the order is violated;
- an exact scenario is repeated;
- human observations are fabricated or substituted;
- human and system time are conflated;
- the operator inspects the hidden answer key before completing the session;
- the corpus, conditions, metrics, or scoring rules change after session
  start; or
- required raw observations are unavailable.

No invalid measurement may be converted to a substantive learning result.

## 17. Bounded PEX measurement authorization

R6.1 authorizes measurement only for PEX-02, PEX-05, and PEX-06. PEX-01,
PEX-03, and PEX-04 remain not measured.

### 17.1 PEX-02

Measure only system time for governed candidate, review, and decision
operations. Exclude human decision time. The target is P95 no greater than
2,000 ms. Report sample size and calculation method.

### 17.2 PEX-05

Measure system time from detection to visible held/fail-closed status. Exclude
human acknowledgement and resolution time. The target is P95 no greater than
1,000 ms. Reasons must remain visible and authorize no action.

### 17.3 PEX-06

Run exactly 100 sequential bounded harness operations. The target requires:

- no unexpected repository or storage mutation;
- no unauthorized authority transition;
- no crash;
- no unhandled exception; and
- no silent loss of status/audit result.

This is a bounded stability result, not production reliability.
